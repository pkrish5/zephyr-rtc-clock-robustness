## Prespecified comparison table

For each of the three model cohorts and the matched upstream baseline, report: (1) generated/attempted, build, nominal and functional attrition under both oracle gates; (2) source-level `unjustified`, `none_found`, and `unknown` timing premises per task and over all sources; (3) within-version pass-to-fail counts and Wilson intervals over evaluable seeded schedules; (4) task-level mean flip fraction over functionally eligible versions, with missing/unevaluable tasks explicit; (5) paired same-seed disagreements against the upstream selected case and between eligible model versions where a task supports it; (6) four-way flip-cause totals and unreplayed/unknown cases. Timing-bound-inadequate but functionally adequate tests remain in the primary arm. Report each task, the ten HSI tasks as a stratum, and the single selected RTC task separately. Do not use one selected RTC task as a model-wide prevalence estimate.

Primary descriptive model contrasts are Luna–Sol, Luna–Astra, and Sol–Astra on matched task-level functional yield, source timing-assumption rate, and task-level flip fraction. Show raw differences with task-level uncertainty; the five generated suites within a task and 100 schedules within a suite are clustered, so naive pooled-suite or pooled-seed intervals are not evidence of independent projects. A task-cluster bootstrap over ten HSI tasks may describe uncertainty but is exploratory at this sample size; the RTC task is presented separately without cross-task inference. If formal p-values are reported for the three model contrasts, adjust the family with Holm's method and disclose multiplicity. Report negative findings and undefined contrasts when no eligible suite exists. The upstream tests are repository-maintained comparison cases, not verified individually human-authored tests.
