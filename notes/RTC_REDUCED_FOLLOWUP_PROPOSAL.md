## Reference-oracle triage

One manually written ztest source per task is saved under [`subjects/reference_rtc`](../subjects/reference_rtc). The tests check the registered calendar transition from 2024-12-31 23:59:55 to 2025-01-01 00:00:05. Callback checks both alarm IDs and one callback per channel; counting checks monotonic calendar progression and midnight crossing; pending checks the alarm pending bit. Each uses an observable completion condition, an early check, and a finite ceiling. These sources are **uncompiled drafts**, awaiting a build and smoke check after approval. Their test and ELF hashes will be frozen before the triage runs. If a reference test cannot build or pass at nominal and both endpoints, stop triage for that task and report the issue; do not silently revise and rerun.

With prescaler `128 × 250`, the ideal 10-RTC-second transition at 17 kHz is `18.823529` host seconds. Allow one calendar-tick phase (`1.882353` seconds) and five host seconds for experimental service, then round the sum up to a **26-second** ceiling. At 47 kHz, nine full periods after the set-time phase give a conservative earliest transition of `9 × 32000 / 47000 = 6.127660` seconds; the code uses a 6125-ms lower check to allow uptime quantization. Both margins are explicit analysis choices, not datasheet guarantees for IRQ or thread delivery. The reference callback captures its RTC calendar at callback time, plus host uptime, to check early firing. If this ISR-time read proves unsafe or perturbs delivery, triage remains unresolved pending review.

For each task, run its reference test at the two legal LSI endpoints and at the **union of registered seed IDs with an original assertion failure in that task** across AI and human measured suites. Do not run a seed solely because a prediction-only suite forecasts failure. Endpoints stay outside seeded-rate denominators. Do not substitute reference verdicts or fresh diagnostic runs for original records. Save source/ELF hash, simulator version, seed, LSI setting, monitor/UART, and trace, and replay reference failures.

Triage decision: when the reference passes at the matching seed, an original failure that replays identically and whose source assertion contradicts the written clock bound remains `brittle_test_assumption`. A passing reference by itself is insufficient to classify a different failure. A reference failure, divergent replay, setup error, or conflicting trace makes the case `unknown` until firmware and peripheral evidence distinguishes genuine bug from model artifact. Review changed classifications as a separate before/after table; retain the original ledger and reasons. This is post-outcome triangulation and does not make the reference an independent physical oracle.

## Timing-mutant quality check

Run exactly three fault operators for **each** of the callback, counting, and pending tasks, at **nominal 32-kHz LSI only**. The intended operators are: (i) no calendar/alarm progression or delivery (`never fires`), (ii) an effective RTC progression slower than the 17-kHz legal minimum such that the target arrives **later** than the legal maximum, and (iii) an effective progression faster than the 47-kHz legal maximum such that the target arrives **earlier** than the legal minimum. A candidate implementation uses isolated Renode RTC clock/fault variants against the frozen suite sources; this is **fault injection**, deliberately outside the legal schedule envelope, and its outcomes are not RQ2 legal-schedule flip rates. Validate each mutant's activated behavior with a separate trace before scoring. The specific implementation and hashes must be frozen after a pilot; if an operator cannot be activated without breaking setup or creating a harness error, report it as invalid and seek review rather than replacing it.

Each of the 66 functionally eligible AI RTC suites and the three selected upstream human tests receives the three mutants for its own task: `19+1` callback, `27+1` counting, and `20+1` pending, for **207 nominal mutant executions** (60 + 84 + 63). A mutant is killed only when the unchanged test passes nominally on the reference setup and fails a meaningful assertion on the activated mutant. A compile failure, test setup failure, and harness error are separate unevaluable outcomes; report coverage and per-operator kills. Report kill count / evaluable mutants by model × prompt × task, plus model × prompt summaries; retain upstream per-task comparators. The intended discrimination is that early, missing, and too-late behavior should each be detected; a broad timeout or false positive is not automatically credited. The mutant arm has different, intentionally faulty device behavior and must be labeled separately from the legal LSI experiment.

## Run-time estimate before approval

At the current original-record snapshot, distinct failed seeds are **26 callback, 17 counting, and 26 pending**; the eventual unions may grow. The reduced triage therefore currently implies `26+17+26 + 3×2 = 75` reference runs, at most `3×(100+2)=306` if every registered seed fails somewhere in every task. Existing RTC v2 seeded records have about **46.3 seconds median wall time**; 75 × 46.3 seconds is **0.96 serial machine-hours**, plus three builds (~12 seconds each), validation, and replay of any reference failures. With up to three independent Renode processes, expect about **25–60 minutes wall time** at the current union, or up to roughly **2–5 hours** at the all-seed upper bound, depending on contention and reference-test runtime. These are estimates, not schedules or a completion promise.

For mutation, existing factorial RTC builds have a **12.3-second median** and nominal simulator passes a **38.3-second median**. `207×(12.3+38.3)` is **2.91 serial machine-hours** of measured-type work; up to three independent workers imply an ideal floor near **one hour**. Allow **1.5–3 hours wall time** for builds, process contention, fault activation checks, and result audit, plus any engineering time to implement/verify the fault operators. Invalid mutants may require review and increase this estimate. No mutant run is authorized by this estimate alone.

## Analysis limits

The range-present prompt adds a documented clock paragraph and a warning that clock bounds do not guarantee ISR-to-thread latency. Any observed context effect cannot be separated from a generic timing-reminder effect. A reminder-only prompt control is future work. Keep the task, rather than the seed or generated suite, as the main comparative cluster; the three RTC tasks share one board, RTC driver, and clock model, so cluster-aware intervals with only three tasks will be wide and exploratory. Report per-task results and descriptive contrasts before pooled estimates.
