case vectors at all 612 shared settings. H753's legal range (29,760-33,600 Hz) is

Case counts per board (F4, F767, F746, H753): (a) 1, 1, 1, 0; (b) 3, 3, 2, 0;
(c) 1, 1, 0, 0; (d) 1, 1, 1, 0. Failure counts: (a) 81, (b) 196, (c) 88, (d) 105,
total 470. F746 has no counter application; its counter was excluded earlier for
MPU-fault incomplete applications.

Reasoning:
- `test_alarm` (first case in the suite): identical to isolated; brackets the
  zero-phase prediction 320000/13 = 24,615 Hz.
- `test_alarm_callback`: 3 + 10 s of sleeps. The source band is 22,154-24,615 Hz;
  the measured transition is inside it and lower than isolated.
- `test_time_counting`: last of 30 polls at 14.5 s. Band 19,862-22,069 Hz; same
  pattern. Mechanism sources: `FULL_APPLICATION_PHASE.md`,
  `model_source/{STM32F4_RTC.cs,LimitTimer.cs}`.
- `test_all_channels` (`test_counter.c:692-701`): 2 requested ticks plus 1 added
  by `counter_ll_stm32_rtc.c` ("Add +1 in order to compensate the partially started
  tick"), then a 3 s busy wait. Band 21,333-32,000 Hz; measured inside it. The phase
  comes from the free-running tick, not a calendar write, so explain it separately.
- `test_single_shot_alarm_notop`: fails in 0.002 s with `err: -16` at `test_counter.c:475`, at
  exactly the `test_all_channels` settings; the driver returns -EBUSY when a callback
  is still registered (`counter_ll_stm32_rtc.c:520-523`). The assertion skips cleanup.
- `test_seconds_rate`: its stated purpose is checking roughly 1 Hz, within a 1.2 s
  sleep. Transition brackets 32000/1.2 = 26,667 Hz. Single boundary, but per
  `notes/s2_qualification_2026-10-26_resume/INTERPRETATION_GUARDS.md` it must not
  be labeled a waiting defect or spurious without a defined clock-accuracy contract.

Reconciliation with isolated runs (`results/rtc_factorial_v2/records/human-*.json`;
same 102 settings, F4 only, 306 runs, no errors):

| Case | Isolated last fail / first pass | Differing settings, isolated fail and full pass |
|---|---|---|
| `test_alarm` | 24,428 / 24,734 | none (0 of 102) |
| `test_alarm_callback` | 24,428 / 24,734 | 23,847; 23,876; 24,346; 24,428 |
| `test_time_counting` | 21,782 / 22,405 | 20,701; 21,402; 21,742; 21,762; 21,782 |

No differences go the other way. Likely reason: earlier cases leave the RTC partway
