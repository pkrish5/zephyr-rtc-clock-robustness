## Working distinction for the paper

The verified work establishes three adjacent lines: general flaky-test causes and `waitFor` repairs (Luo et al.); embedded LLM development and test generation (EmbedAgent, EmbC-Test); and interrupt-aware firmware analysis/fuzzing (Regehr, SDRacer, AidFuzzer, AIM). Berndt et al. already study LLM-generated test flakiness in DBMSs. Thus the defensible research question is narrower: whether **frozen agent-written firmware tests** and matched upstream tests have timing-dependent verdict changes under **documented, replayable hardware-clock variation**, with each change checked for oracle inadequacy, firmware races, and model artifacts. It is an empirical question; current Phase 3 RTC agent tests have failed the oracle-adequacy gate, so they cannot establish a comparative robustness advantage. Avoid “first” or effectiveness claims until the full corpus and validation support them.
